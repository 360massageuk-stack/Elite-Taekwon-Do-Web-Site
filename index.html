Who We Are

WORLD-CLASS
TAEKWON-DO

Family-friendly ITF Taekwon-Do classes from age 3 to adult, across multiple locations throughout Scotland.

Elite Scotland Taekwon-Do is more than a martial arts club — it's a community built on values that shape character and transform lives. Whether you're a complete beginner or looking to push to the next level, our structured classes cater to every stage of the journey.

Every session is carefully designed to develop not just physical skill, but mental resilience, self-confidence, and discipline that carries into every aspect of life.

Start Your Journey Today

What You'll Gain

SIX PILLARS OF
TAEKWON-DO

🥊

Self Defence

Practical, effective techniques taught progressively. Learn to protect yourself and those around you with real confidence.

🏆

Confidence

Watch self-belief grow with every grade. Our students carry their confidence into school, work, and everyday life.

💪

Fitness

Full-body conditioning through dynamic kicking, striking, and pattern work. Better fitness as a natural result of enjoyable training.

🎯

Discipline

Structure and respect are woven into every class. Students learn to set goals, commit to training, and follow through.

🤝

Respect

A core tenet of Taekwon-Do. Students learn to treat instructors, peers, and themselves with genuine respect and humility.

🧠

Focus

Improved concentration and mental clarity. The patterns and techniques of Taekwon-Do train the mind as much as the body.

For Families

KIDS & FAMILY
CLASSES

🥋
Age 3 to Adult Classes for every age group and ability

Our classes are structured, safe, and genuinely fun for children. We start with the fundamentals — balance, coordination, listening skills — and progress through a clear belt grading system that keeps every student motivated.

Parents regularly train alongside their children. Training together as a family is one of the most powerful things Taekwon-Do can offer — shared goals, shared discipline, shared achievements.

All classes are run by fully certified, PVG-checked instructors. Your child's safety and enjoyment is our priority from day one.
