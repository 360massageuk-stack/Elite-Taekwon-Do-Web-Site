Martial Arts in Edinburgh

If you're looking for martial arts classes in Edinburgh, you may be considering Taekwon-Do, Karate, Kickboxing or Judo. Each martial art has its own strengths and benefits.

Why choose ITF Taekwon-Do?

Elite Taekwon-Do Scotland teaches traditional ITF Taekwon-Do for children, teenagers and adults. Training develops:

  • Confidence
  • Fitness
  • Flexibility
  • Self-defence
  • Discipline
  • Respect
  • Goal setting

Is Taekwon-Do better than Karate?

There isn't one martial art that is best for everyone. Karate, Kickboxing, Judo and Taekwon-Do all have different training styles. Many families choose ITF Taekwon-Do because it combines practical self-defence, traditional values, fitness and an excellent structured grading system.

Children's Martial Arts

Our children's classes help improve confidence, concentration, coordination and respect while keeping training enjoyable and challenging.

Adult Beginners Welcome

Many adults believe they are too old to start martial arts. The truth is that beginners of all ages are welcome and many of our students started later in life.
