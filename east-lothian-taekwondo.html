TRAIN WITH CLAIRE

Claire is a female Elite Scotland Taekwon-Do instructor teaching local classes in this area. Her sessions are suitable for complete beginners as well as existing students, with a strong focus on confidence, technique, fitness and progression at each student’s own level.

New students

BEGINNERS ARE WELCOME

You do not need to be fit, flexible or experienced before you start. Taekwon-Do training develops those qualities gradually. Children can build coordination and confidence, while adults can train for fitness, self-defence, a new challenge or simply something enjoyable to do each week.

Do I need experience?

No. Complete beginners are welcome and will be shown the fundamentals from the start.

Can adults start Taekwon-Do?

Yes. Adults can begin at any level of fitness and progress at their own pace.

Is there a class for younger children?

Yes. Danderhall has Ninja Kickers for ages 3–7, and Gullane welcomes students from age 5.

Can we try before joining?

Yes. Use the free-trial enquiry on the main Elite Scotland website and tell us which local class you would like to visit.
